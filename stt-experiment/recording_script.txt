STT EXPERIMENT — RECORDING SCRIPT
=================================

HOW TO RECORD
-------------
- Record one clip per line below. Speak naturally, at the pace you'd use
  when actually talking to the app.
- Save each clip as WAV, 16 kHz mono if you can (any WAV works).
- Name the files EXACTLY p01.wav ... p15.wav and put them in:
      stt-experiment/speech_files/
- The reference text the WER is scored against is the line after each ID.
  Read it verbatim (the experiment normalises case/punctuation, so don't
  worry about commas — just say the words in order).
- Quick way to record on Mac:
      QuickTime Player > File > New Audio Recording  (then export)
  or, from the terminal with sox installed:
      rec -r 16000 -c 1 speech_files/p01.wav

WHY THESE LINES
---------------
They are deliberately entity-dense: real business names, target audiences,
goals and calls-to-action. Smaller Whisper models tend to mangle proper
nouns and rare words while still scoring "okay" on common words — these
clips expose exactly that failure mode, which is the interesting result.
